The band

“ Good country music, a little cowboy spirit, and a damn good time ”

Hoss and the Hangmen bring a distinctly Colorado spirit to the Northern Colorado music scene, blending traditional country, outlaw influences, and modern Americana. Fronted by Hoss, a guitar-slinging singing cowboy, the group pairs storytelling, rugged melodies, and an unpretentious approach to live performances. The Hangmen join Hoss for a duo or a full 3-man lineup allowing the band to move comfortably between small, intimate solo performances to energetic shows that fill the room. But in any case, the mission is the same: good songs, engaging performances, and plenty of grit and personality.

The set list is mostly covers, with a handful of original songs mixed in, drawing from artists like Johnny Cash, Garth Brooks, Colter Wall, Zach Bryan, and Trampled by Turtles.

  • Ben (Hoss) guitar + vocals
  • Anthony upright bass
  • Alex drums
