Hoss and the Hangmen

“ Good country music, a little cowboy spirit, and a damn good time ”

Hoss and the Hangmen bring together classic country, modern Americana, and folk, blending familiar favorites with a handful of original songs inspired by the same roots. Drawing from artists like Johnny Cash, Garth Brooks, Colter Wall, Zach Bryan, and Trampled by Turtles, the band delivers a mix of familiar songs, storytelling, and toe-tapping rhythms. And all with a heavy pour of good old-fashioned Western country spirit.

Featuring acoustic guitar and vocals, upright bass, and drums, Hoss and the Hangmen bring an engaging performance with a little cowboy flair. Whether it's a rowdy beer garden, a lively brewery, or an intimate cocktail lounge, the band adapts its sound and energy to fit the room. Based in Northern Colorado, they perform throughout the region as a full trio, acoustic duo, or solo act. Easygoing, family-friendly, and professional, they're focused on making every show a great experience for audiences and venues alike.
